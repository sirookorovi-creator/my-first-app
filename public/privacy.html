Privacy & data — MVP

This is a technical beta policy draft. The deployer must add their identity, contact address, legal basis and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before any public launch.

What is processed

When you record or type a message, the audio or text is sent to the app server and then to OpenAI's API for transcription and translation. OpenAI may process the content under its applicable API terms. The app does not persist uploaded audio recordings. It stores the original transcript, translated text and timestamps in Cloudflare D1 to display the shared conversation. Messages may contain personal data.

Retention and deletion

Rooms expire seven days after creation. Expired rooms and messages are deleted during a daily cleanup task. The room creator can delete the room at any time. Technical rate-limit records are maintained briefly using salted hashes of IP addresses.

Who can read messages

Anyone with a valid conversation link can read that room and reply. Links are bearer secrets. Do not use the beta for passwords, medical decisions, legal instructions, financial transactions or confidential materials. The service does not provide end-to-end encryption, authentication or message-recall guarantees.

AI errors

Translation is automated and may change meaning. Confirm important dates, names, amounts and instructions independently.

External services

The app uses Cloudflare Workers/D1 for service operation and the OpenAI API for AI processing. Browser speech synthesis is used for playback, subject to your device's capabilities and settings.
